Essential Travel Tips

Expert advice to make your Mumbai adventure smooth and enjoyable

💧
Stay Hydrated
Only drink bottled water. Carry water bottles everywhere. India can be hot and humid, especially for kids. Avoid ice in drinks unless at reputable hotels. Coconut water is excellent and safe!
🍽️
Food Safety
Stick to cooked foods, avoid street food initially until your stomach adjusts. Peel fruits yourself. Choose busy restaurants (high turnover = fresh food). Most hotels offer mild options for kids.
🚕
Transportation
Use Uber/Ola apps for reliable transport. Pre-book airport transfers. For long journeys, hire a driver for the day (safer with kids). Always negotiate auto-rickshaw fares beforehand.
💰
Money Matters
Carry cash (small denominations). ATMs widely available but notify your bank. Credit cards accepted in major establishments. Keep emergency cash separate. Tipping: 10% at restaurants.
👗
Dress Code
Dress modestly at religious sites (shoulders and knees covered). Remove shoes when entering temples. Light, breathable fabrics recommended. Kids can dress comfortably but modestly.
📱
Stay Connected
Buy local SIM card at airport (Airtel/Jio). Need passport photo and copy. Data is cheap and coverage good. Download offline maps. Share live location with family when separated.
🏥
Health Precautions
Vaccinations: Hepatitis A, Typhoid recommended. Malaria risk low in cities but take precautions. Travel insurance mandatory. Know nearest hospital location. Carry basic medicines from home.
⏰
Time Management
Traffic can be unpredictable. Start early to beat crowds and heat. Build in downtime for kids. Most attractions close on Mondays. Book popular sites online in advance to skip queues.
🛡️
Safety First
Mumbai is generally safe for tourists. Watch belongings in crowded areas. Use hotel safe for valuables. Hold kids' hands in busy markets. Be cautious of overly friendly strangers.
🌡️
Weather Wisdom
Best time: November-February (cooler, dry). Expect 25-30°C temperatures. High humidity in Mumbai. Goa can be hot. Carry light layers. Check monsoon season (June-September) if traveling then.
🎁
Shopping Smart
Bargaining expected at markets (start at 50% asking price). Fixed prices at malls. Best buys: textiles, handicrafts, spices, jewelry. Keep receipts for customs. Pack fragile items carefully.
🤝
Cultural Respect
Indians love children! Expect attention. Use right hand for giving/receiving. Ask before photographing people. Learn basic Hindi phrases (Namaste, Dhanyavaad). Respect local customs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you go

Do we need visas for India? ▼
Yes, Belgian citizens need an e-Visa for India. Apply online at least 4 days before travel (recommended 2 weeks ahead). Tourist e-Visa valid for 90 days, multiple entry. Cost: approximately $80 per person. Children need their own visa. Process takes 2-4 business days.
Is India safe for families with young children? ▼
Absolutely! India is very family-friendly and locals adore children. Millions of families visit annually. Main concerns are food/water safety (easily managed) and traffic (use reliable transport). Tourist areas in Mumbai and Goa are well-developed and safe. This itinerary focuses on family-friendly destinations with excellent infrastructure.
What vaccinations do we need? ▼
Recommended vaccinations: Hepatitis A, Hepatitis B, Typhoid, Tetanus, and routine vaccinations (MMR, etc.). Consult your doctor 6-8 weeks before travel. Malaria prophylaxis not essential for Mumbai/Goa but discuss with doctor. Bring prescription medications and basic first aid supplies.
Can kids handle the food in India? ▼
Yes! Indian cuisine offers many mild options perfect for children. Request "no spicy" at restaurants. Options include: naan bread, rice dishes, butter chicken (mild), paneer dishes, dosas, idlis, pasta at hotels, and familiar fast food chains. Most hotels offer continental breakfast. Bring favorite snacks from home for picky eaters.
What's the best way to get around? ▼
For Mumbai: Use Uber/Ola apps for reliable rides. Metro system is modern and efficient. For day trips: hire a private driver (affordable and convenient with kids). Between cities: domestic flights save time (Mumbai-Goa-Aurangabad). In Goa: rent a scooter (for parents) or hire driver. Pre-booking is recommended for peace of mind.
What about internet and mobile connectivity? ▼
India has excellent mobile coverage. Buy a local SIM at the airport (Jio or Airtel recommended). You'll need: passport copy, passport photo, local address. Data is very affordable (20-30GB for ~€10). Most hotels offer WiFi. Download offline maps and entertainment for kids before data-heavy travel days.
Is €11,000 enough for this trip? ▼
Yes, this budget is realistic and comfortable for 5 people for 21 days! It includes flights, accommodation, food, activities, and transport with €500 emergency buffer. You can even save money by: eating at local restaurants occasionally, booking accommodations with kitchenettes, using public transport sometimes. Budget allows for comfortable 3-4 star stays and regular activities.
What's the best time to visit? ▼
Best period: November to February (winter season). Weather is pleasant (20-28°C), minimal rain, comfortable for sightseeing. December-January is peak tourist season (book early!). March-May is hot (35°C+). June-September is monsoon (heavy rain, not ideal). For your trip, aim for November-February for optimal family comfort.
Do hotels have family rooms? ▼
Yes! Many hotels offer family rooms or connecting rooms. Options include: family suites, apartments with multiple bedrooms, rooms with extra beds, or two adjoining rooms. Beach resorts in Goa often have family-friendly cottages. Always mention you have 3 children when booking. Many hotels provide cribs for the 4-year-old at no extra charge.
What should we avoid in India? ▼
Avoid: tap water (drink only bottled), ice from unknown sources, raw salads initially, street food until acclimatized, showing excessive wealth, isolated areas at night, aggressive touts (politely decline), monsoon season travel. Do: stay hydrated, use sunscreen, follow hotel food recommendations, keep copies of documents, maintain hygiene, and enjoy the adventure!
